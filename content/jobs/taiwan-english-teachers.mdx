Candidates can arrive in Taiwan visa exempt and could be there within a few weeks of receiving an offer. Training and induction are provided before any teaching begins, with meals and accommodation provided during the initial training.

**Requirements:** a bachelor's degree or a US Associate's degree. Candidates with a US Associate's degree need to have or obtain a TEFL certificate; those with a bachelor's degree do not. Candidates must have a hard copy of their degree certificate, must be able to obtain a clean national criminal background check, and must have a passport with at least 18 months before expiry. The school is looking for enthusiastic and dynamic graduates with a genuine love of working with children, and candidates must be flexible enough to work with children of all ages.

**Salary and benefits:** hourly pay starts at NT$650 to 700 per hour, depending on previous teaching qualifications and experience, with a ceiling rate from NT$800 per hour.

**Teaching duties stipend:** NT$3,000 per month for class preparation work, such as meetings, training and lesson preparation.

**First-year contract renewal bonus:** staff retention is an important priority for the school, and the purpose of this bonus is to encourage teachers to stay for at least two years (contracts are for a year). NT$10,000 is paid in the sixth month of the second contract, and NT$15,000 at the end of the 24th month.

**Approximate monthly income, depending on hours worked:** 26 hours a week comes to about 75,600 NTD per month.
