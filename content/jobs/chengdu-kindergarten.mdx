**Requirements:** a passion for children's education and child development; a bachelor's degree from a recognised institution; no criminal record; eligible for a work Z visa.

**Working conditions and benefits:** a three-in-one teaching team, with a dual-class teacher system for both Chinese and English (one full-time foreign teacher, two Chinese teachers and one life teacher).

**Exploratory theme-based teaching curriculum:** the children carry out in-depth research on topics of interest from their own lives, or on questions they are curious about, with support from their teachers. It is a curriculum reform that moves away from traditional subject-based teaching towards a more democratic and collaborative way of building knowledge.
