We are a boutique English language school for young children in downtown Shanghai. We started our school in 2013 to improve the quality of after-school education.

**A couple of things that set us apart:** small class sizes of 4 to 8 students to ensure quality English education; continued professional and personal development; a close-knit team of teachers and plenty of team-building events and trips; free Chinese language classes; and a central location in Huangpu district.

**The job:** based in Huangpu district, downtown Shanghai, teaching the Longman Pearson curriculum, which includes Tot Talk, Welcome to English and Side by Side. We also offer reading and writing classes, arts and crafts, science and drama classes.

**Pay and benefits:** the after-tax salary of 20,000 to 25,000 RMB per month (3,000 to 3,500 USD) depends on experience, and is for teachers with one to three years of relevant teaching experience with students aged 4 to 12. In practice our teachers are paid around 30,000 to 32,000 RMB a month after tax once housing allowance, overtime and performance bonuses are counted.

**Requirements:** a commitment to a one-year contract; a willingness to learn about, coach and give constructive feedback to new teachers; a willingness to learn about course and curriculum development; a clear criminal background check; two years of work experience; and a love of interacting with kids.
