**Location and number of positions:** Harbin, 1; Jinan, 3; Qinghuangdao, 2; Qingdao, 1; Taizhou and Nanjing, 1.

**Responsibilities:** deliver English for Academic Purposes programmes to Chinese university students who may transfer to universities in Australia, New Zealand and the UK; carry out administrative duties promptly and accurately, including recording attendance and giving written feedback on student assessments; address individual students' needs and concerns inside and outside the classroom; take an active part in professional development with local teachers at branches in China, and encourage it; attend weekly branch meetings; help create a team environment and contribute to the delivery of quality English language courses.

**Workload:** 38 hours a week, made up of 20 to 21 hours of face-to-face teaching and 17 to 18 office hours. Office hours are for lesson planning, marking, student tutorials and similar work, and can be flexible.

**Requirements:** a teaching certificate such as CELTA, TEFL or TESOL.
